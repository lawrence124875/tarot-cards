塔羅 隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 12 日
開發者：LC Lab（Lawrence Chang）　聯絡信箱：lawrence124875@gmail.com

「塔羅」（以下稱「本 App」）是以七十八張塔羅牌為題材的學習與自我反思工具。本 App 不需要註冊或登入，牌義解讀全部預先寫在 App 裡，不使用 AI 服務，也不會把您寫下的任何文字傳到伺服器。本政策說明本 App 會蒐集哪些資訊、如何使用，以及您擁有的權利。

只存在您手機裡的資料

  • 塔羅日記：您保存的每次抽牌（牌陣、抽到的牌）、寫下的「想問的事」、「第一印象」筆記、事後的回顧筆記，以及回顧提醒的時間，只儲存在您的裝置本機。開發者看不到，App 也不會自動上傳、備份或分享。您可以在日記列表右上角選「備份」，自行把備份檔存到您選擇的地方（例如您的雲端硬碟），日後用「從備份還原」取回；備份檔不會經過開發者。解除安裝本 App 或清除 App 資料而沒有備份時，這些內容會一併刪除且無法復原。
  • 設定：牌面組的選擇、是否使用逆位、「今日一張」提醒時間，以及呼吸練習的設定（時長、鈴聲與雙耳節拍開關等），同樣只存在本機。
  • 其他使用紀錄：當天的「今日一張」、認牌小測驗的進度，以及免費版當日已抽牌的次數，也只存在本機。

為改善 App 而蒐集的資訊

  • 匿名使用統計（Firebase Analytics）：記錄少數操作事件，以了解功能是否好用，例如：顯示了一次解讀（抽牌方式、牌陣、主要牌的編號 0–77、正位或逆位）、保存了一則塔羅日記（是否設定提醒）、開始或完成一次呼吸練習（牌的編號、分鐘數、是否開啟無聲引導或雙耳節拍）。Firebase Analytics 也會自動收集一般的裝置與使用資訊（例如裝置型號、系統版本、App 版本、大致地區、使用時段），並使用 App 實例識別碼等裝置識別碼。
    統計絕不包含您寫下的「想問的事」、第一印象、回顧筆記或任何您輸入的文字。
  • 當機報告（Firebase Crashlytics）：App 發生當機時，蒐集錯誤堆疊、裝置型號、系統版本等診斷資訊，用於修正問題，不包含您的個人身分資訊或日記內容。
  • 可以關閉：首頁右上角選單取消勾選「分享匿名使用統計與當機報告」，App 就立即停止蒐集上述使用統計與當機報告（廣告的資料處理另見下方「廣告」段）。
  • 功能設定（Firebase Remote Config）：App 會定期下載功能開關設定（例如暫時關閉某項功能），此過程會使用 App 實例識別碼，不傳送您的個人資料。

廣告（免費版）

免費版會在首頁與日記列表底部顯示橫幅廣告、看完解讀回到上一頁時偶爾顯示插頁廣告，並提供您自行選擇觀看的獎勵廣告（看完可多抽一次牌，或在塔羅日記達免費上限時多存一則）。呼吸練習與閱讀解讀時不會跳出廣告。廣告由 Google AdMob 提供，AdMob（Google Mobile Ads SDK）可能蒐集下列資料：

  • 裝置或其他 ID：Android 廣告 ID（AAID）、App 組識別碼等。
  • 大略位置：由 IP 位址推得的國家或城市層級位置；本 App 不使用 GPS，也不取得精確位置。
  • 應用程式互動：廣告的曝光、點擊與觀看紀錄，以及 App 啟動等使用資訊。
  • 當機記錄與診斷資料：廣告 SDK 本身的錯誤與效能資訊（例如載入時間、延遲）。
  • 裝置與 App 資訊：裝置型號、系統版本、語言、App 版本。

這些資料用於廣告或行銷（投放廣告、限制頻率；在您同意時顯示個人化廣告）、數據分析（計算廣告成效）以及詐欺防範、安全性與法規遵循（偵測無效流量與廣告詐欺）。開發者只看得到 AdMob 的彙總報表，無法辨識個別使用者。

  • 在需要取得同意的地區（例如歐洲經濟區、英國、瑞士），App 會先以 Google 的同意表單（User Messaging Platform）詢問您，之後可從首頁右上角選單的「廣告隱私設定」隨時更改。
  • 您可在裝置系統設定中重設或刪除廣告識別碼，或選擇停用個人化廣告。
  • 本 App 在 AdMob 封鎖酒精、賭博等敏感類別的廣告，廣告內容分級上限為「家長指導（PG）」。
  • 訂閱會員不顯示任何廣告，也不會載入廣告。

AdMob 的資料處理方式請見 Google 如何使用合作夥伴網站或應用程式的資訊。

訂閱（塔羅會員）

月訂與年訂經由 Google Play 結帳完成，付款資料（信用卡等）由 Google 處理，開發者看不到。訂閱狀態透過 RevenueCat 服務確認：RevenueCat 會收到一組由 App 隨機產生的匿名使用者 ID（格式如 $RCAnonymousID:…，與您的 Google 帳戶、姓名或電子郵件無關）、購買記錄（方案、價格與幣別、購買與到期時間、Google Play 訂單編號與購買憑證）以及裝置與 App 基本資訊，只用於確認您是否為會員、恢復購買與訂閱統計，不含您的姓名或電子郵件。訂閱會自動續訂，可隨時在 Google Play 的「付款和訂閱」中取消，取消後會員資格保留到本期結束。RevenueCat 的資料處理方式請見 RevenueCat 隱私權政策。

意見回饋

首頁右上角選單的「意見回饋」由您主動填寫並按下送出才會傳送，內容包括：您選的類型（回報問題／功能建議／其他）、您寫的內容、選填的聯絡信箱，以及 App 版本、介面語言、平台（Android 或 iOS）與送出時間。資料存放在 Google 的 Firebase Cloud Firestore，只有開發者能在後台查看，App 本身無法讀取任何人的回饋。用途只限於了解問題、改善 App，以及在您留下信箱時回覆您；不會用於廣告或寄送行銷信件。意見回饋不會附上您的塔羅日記或想問的事；請勿在內容中填寫不想讓開發者看到的個人資料。

App 使用的權限

  • 通知、準時鬧鐘、開機後重新排程：只用於您自行設定的提醒——「塔羅日記回顧提醒」（在您選定的日子提醒您回來看這次抽牌），以及選擇開啟的「今日一張」每日提醒（在您選定的時間提醒您抽今天的牌）。
  • 前景服務（媒體播放）、保持喚醒：只用於呼吸音景，讓您關閉螢幕或切到其他 App 時音景能繼續播放，並在鎖定畫面顯示暫停、停止鍵。
  • 網路、廣告識別碼：用於上述 Firebase 服務、顯示廣告（免費版）與確認訂閱狀態。
  • Google Play 結帳：只用於購買與恢復訂閱。

本 App 不會做的事

  • 不要求註冊帳號或登入。
  • 不蒐集姓名、電話、電子郵件、地址、相片、聯絡人或精確位置（您在意見回饋中自願留下的聯絡信箱除外）。
  • 不出售或出租任何使用者資料。
  • 不使用 AI 服務分析您的提問或筆記。

資料分享

上述統計、當機、設定與意見回饋資料由 Google 的 Firebase 服務處理；廣告由 Google AdMob 處理；訂閱由 Google Play 與 RevenueCat 處理（見上方各段）。傳輸過程皆經過加密。除此之外，本 App 不與任何第三方分享資料，也絕不分享您的塔羅日記與輸入的文字。Firebase 的資料處理方式請見 Firebase 隱私權與安全性說明。

兒童隱私

本 App 並非為 13 歲以下兒童設計，也不會刻意向兒童蒐集個人資訊。

內容性質

本 App 的牌義與解讀僅供自我探索與學習參考，不構成任何醫療、心理、法律、財務或其他專業建議，也不保證任何預測結果。如果您正經歷困難的時刻，請向信任的人或專業人士求助。

  • 訂閱相關資料（RevenueCat）：請來信 lawrence124875@gmail.com，主旨註明「塔羅 資料刪除請求」，並附上 Google Play 訂單編號（GPA. 開頭，可在 Google Play「付款和訂閱 → 預算和記錄」或購買收據信中找到）。我們會在 30 天內向 RevenueCat 提出刪除該匿名使用者 ID 及其購買記錄的請求，完成後回信通知。刪除後如需恢復會員，可在 App 內按「恢復購買」重新建立。

Tarot · LC Lab Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026
Developer: LC Lab (Lawrence Chang) · Contact: lawrence124875@gmail.com

Tarot · LC Lab (“the App”) is a learning and self-reflection tool based on the 78 tarot cards. The App requires no account or sign-in. All card meanings and readings are written in advance and bundled in the App; no AI service is used, and nothing you type is sent to any server. This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your rights.

Data that stays on your phone

  • Tarot journal: the readings you save (spread and cards drawn), the question you wrote, your “first impression” notes, your follow-up notes and review-reminder times are stored only on your device. The developer cannot see them, and the App never uploads, backs up or shares them by itself. You can choose “Back up” in the journal list menu to save a backup file wherever you choose (for example your own cloud drive), and later bring it back with “Restore from backup”; the backup file never passes through the developer. Without a backup, uninstalling the App or clearing its data deletes them permanently.
  • Settings, such as your deck choice, whether reversals are used, the daily-card reminder time, and breathing settings (session length, sound options, binaural beats), are also stored only on your device.
  • Other usage records: today’s card of the day, your card-quiz progress, and the number of free draws used today are also stored only on your device.

Information collected to improve the App

  • Anonymous usage statistics (Firebase Analytics): a few events that show how features are used — a reading was shown (draw method, spread, key card number 0–77, upright or reversed), a journal entry was saved (whether a reminder was set), a breathing session was started or completed (card number, minutes, whether silent guidance or binaural beats were on). Firebase Analytics also automatically collects general device and usage information (such as device model, OS version, App version, approximate region and session times) and uses device identifiers such as the app instance ID.
    Statistics never include your question, your first impressions, your notes or any text you type.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): when the App crashes, diagnostic information such as the stack trace, device model and OS version is collected to fix the problem. It contains no personal identity information or journal content.
  • You can turn this off: untick “Share anonymous usage stats & crash reports” in the home screen menu and the App immediately stops collecting the usage statistics and crash reports above (advertising data is covered separately under “Ads” below).
  • Feature settings (Firebase Remote Config): the App periodically downloads feature switches. This uses the app instance ID and sends no personal data.

Ads (free version)

The free version shows a banner at the bottom of the home screen and journal list, occasionally shows an interstitial ad when you return from a reading, and offers optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for one extra draw, or to save one more journal entry after reaching the free limit. No ads appear during breathing sessions or while you read. Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK), which may collect:

  • Device or other IDs: the Android advertising ID (AAID) and app set ID.
  • Approximate location: country- or city-level location derived from your IP address. The App does not use GPS or collect precise location.
  • App interactions: ad impressions, clicks and views, and usage information such as App launches.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics: errors and performance data from the ads SDK itself (for example load times and latency).
  • Device and app information: device model, OS version, language and App version.

This data is used for advertising or marketing (serving ads, frequency capping and, with your consent, personalized ads), analytics (measuring ad performance), and fraud prevention, security and compliance (detecting invalid traffic and ad fraud). The developer only sees aggregated AdMob reports and cannot identify individual users.

  • Where consent is required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the App first asks using Google’s consent form (User Messaging Platform); you can change your choice at any time under “Ad privacy settings” in the menu at the top right of the home screen.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings.
  • Sensitive ad categories such as alcohol and gambling are blocked, and ad content is limited to the PG rating.
  • Premium members see no ads, and no ads are loaded for them.

See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Subscription (Tarot Premium)

Monthly and yearly subscriptions are purchased through Google Play Billing; your payment details are handled by Google and are never visible to the developer. Subscription status is checked through RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous user ID generated at random by the App (such as $RCAnonymousID:…, not linked to your Google account, name or email) and purchase history (plan, price and currency, purchase and expiry times, Google Play order ID and purchase token) and basic device and app information. This is used only to confirm membership, restore purchases and produce subscription statistics, and does not include your name or email address. Subscriptions renew automatically; cancel anytime in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions, and you keep Premium until the end of the current period. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Feedback

“Send feedback” in the home screen menu sends something only when you fill it in and tap send. It includes the type you chose (problem, feature suggestion or other), your message, an optional contact email, and the App version, interface language, platform (Android or iOS) and time sent. It is stored in Google Firebase Cloud Firestore and can be viewed only by the developer in the Firebase console; the App itself cannot read anyone’s feedback. It is used only to understand problems, improve the App and, if you left an email address, reply to you; never for ads or marketing email. Feedback never includes your journal or your questions; please don’t put personal information you don’t want the developer to see in the message.

Permissions

  • Notifications, exact alarms, rescheduling after reboot: used only for reminders you set yourself — journal review reminders, and the optional daily “today’s card” reminder at the time you pick.
  • Foreground service (media playback), wake lock: used only so the breathing soundscape keeps playing with the screen off and shows pause/stop controls on the lock screen.
  • Internet, advertising ID: used for the Firebase services above, for ads in the free version, and for checking subscription status.
  • Google Play Billing: used only to purchase and restore subscriptions.

What the App does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No collection of names, phone numbers, email addresses, home addresses, photos, contacts or precise location (except a contact email you choose to leave with feedback).
  • No selling or renting of user data.
  • No AI analysis of your questions or notes.

Data sharing

The statistics, crash, settings and feedback data above are processed by Google Firebase; ads by Google AdMob; subscriptions by Google Play and RevenueCat (see the sections above). All data is encrypted in transit. The App shares no data with any other third party, and never shares your journal or anything you type. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Children

The App is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Nature of the content

Card meanings and readings are for self-exploration and learning only. They are not medical, psychological, legal, financial or other professional advice, and no prediction is guaranteed. If you are going through a hard time, please reach out to people you trust or to a professional.

Card images: the scans of the original 1909 deck are in the public domain. An optional deck was generated with AI and is labelled as such in the App.

Data deletion

This section applies to the Android app Tarot · LC Lab (塔羅) (package com.lclab.tarot), developed by LC Lab. The App has no accounts, so there is no account to delete. Each type of data is deleted as follows:

  • Journal entries, questions, notes, settings and usage records are stored only on your phone; the developer has no copy. Delete single entries in the App, or uninstall the App (or go to Settings → Apps → Tarot → Storage → Clear data) to delete everything permanently.
  • Advertising ID: in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (on some devices Settings → Google → Ads), choose Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID. After deletion AdMob can no longer link ad data to your device; data AdMob has already collected is handled and retained by Google under its policies.
  • Subscription data (RevenueCat): email lawrence124875@gmail.com with the subject “Tarot data deletion request” and include your Google Play order ID (starting with GPA., found in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history, or in your receipt email). Within 30 days we will ask RevenueCat to delete that anonymous user ID and its purchase history, and reply to confirm. If you later want Premium back, tap “Restore purchases” in the App.
  • Google Play purchase records: payment and order records are retained by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and applicable tax and accounting laws; the developer cannot delete them. Please contact Google for these.
  • Feedback (Firebase Cloud Firestore): email us as above with the subject “Tarot feedback deletion request”, giving roughly when you sent it and what it said (or the email you left). We will delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.
  • Usage statistics and crash reports (Firebase) contain no information that identifies you; Analytics event data is deleted automatically after 14 months. To request earlier deletion of data associated with your device, email us as above.

⚠️ Deleting data or uninstalling the App does not cancel your subscription. Cancel first in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions to stop future charges.
